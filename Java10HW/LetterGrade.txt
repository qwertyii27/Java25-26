This is a summary of the changes I mader, since the PartC Assignment is very long.

First I noticed that the case for changing to 0 and 100 was AND (&&), meaning it would never trigger.
I fixed this by turning it to OR (||), making the min and max actually work.

Next I saw that the pass/fail was ruined by a random semi-colon.
This seperated the conditional from its coding block, making the student always pass.
I removed the random semi-colon.

Third I noticed that the minimum was always showing up as zero.
This was because the initialized minimum was 0, and nothing could be lower.
To fix this, I initialized minimum as 101.

Finally, I noticed that the average would be off because instead of dividing two doubles, the
calculator would first divide the integers and THEN cast them as doubles. I rectified this by
casting both total and count to double before dividing them.
